🔒 Privacy

Short version: your location stays on your device. There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking.

Your location

Your browser gives the page your GPS position, or you type a place. The page uses it to ask for aircraft nearby: it sends a box around you to our server, which asks the free flight-data service adsb.lol. We don't store your location. Your last typed place is remembered in your own browser so the site works when GPS doesn't.

Other free services the page talks to

  • adsbdb: airline, route and aircraft details (sends the aircraft's code, not your location).
  • planespotters.net: aircraft photos (sends the aircraft's code).
  • Open-Meteo: weather for the Airport tab (sends your approximate location).
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: place search and your town's name.
  • Esri map tiles, Google Fonts, cdnjs (map library) and kiwi.com (airline logos).

Like any website, these services see your IP address when your browser loads from them.

What we count

For the public stats page, we count visits per day per country (worked out by Cloudflare from the connection; we never store IP addresses), and how many aircraft were seen per ~100 km area. If the page hits an error, it sends the error message (not your location) so it can be fixed.

Leaderboard

Only if you choose to join: your nickname and the aircraft types you've spotted (like "A388"). Your spotting log itself stays in your browser.

Camera and motion (AR mode)

Used only while AR mode is open, entirely on your phone. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Clearing your data

Everything kept on your device (settings, log, last place) is removed when you clear this site's data in your browser.
